# V1.5 pre-implementation baseline audit

- V1 creates a return request once active energy falls below the launch ratio or the wind-dependent safety boundary; a pure scheduler consumes snapshots and returns launch/return decisions. Projected search count is active minus active pending-return requests, including those already assigned a replacement.
- Request-to-replacement binding is one-to-one. The environment applies it, records events, retains it through return, then releases it. A charged UAV re-enters standby and the next global target-aware decision can launch it when a projected deficit exists.
- Seven of 30 target-aware runs had minimum active count one, but V1 did not include a capacity or target ablation: the cause is not identified. Target-aware mean coverage was roughly 0.99 percentage points below naive, despite a lower deficit; repeated-visit ratio was higher, but V1 did not isolate responsibility, collision or SAGIN contention and cannot establish causation.
- V1 repeated_visit_ratio = (eligible search visits minus newly covered cells)/(eligible search visits), counting a revisit even when a UAV stays at a blocked cell. Coverage starts from the four initial cells already marked searched. Pairwise trajectory conflict, spatial visit overlap and per-step same-target contention are not recorded in V1.
- Replacement-delay means use assigned requests only; target-recovery means use completed gaps only; ongoing recovery and charging waits are right-censored. Requests returned without a replacement were corrected by analysis of stored events, preserving raw counters.
- V1 results use run_id mode/wind/eval seed, fixed wind=wind seed, terrain/infrastructure/trajectory=eval seed, and a combined code/actor-weight SHA-256 in experiment_config.json. Audit verified 90/90. V1.5 must execute new runs and never pool old V1 rows.
- The requested `dynamic_fleet_metrics.py` does not exist; the actual V1 request-accounting module is `fleet_metrics.py`.
